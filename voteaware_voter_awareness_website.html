About the project

Awareness is the first step to participation.

This Community Engagement Project focuses on educating residents—especially first-time voters—about voter registration, polling information, and the importance of informed participation in democracy.

🗳️

Voting matters

Understand how voting lets eligible citizens take part in choosing their representatives.

🪪

Registration basics

Learn about voter registration, checking details, and correcting information through official channels.

📍

Know your booth

Check your assigned polling station and plan ahead using official voter services.

🤝

Community action

Share reliable information respectfully and encourage participation without party bias.

Project activities

From information to action

The report describes these activities as the core of the campaign.

📋

1. Community survey

Ask residents about their awareness of voter registration, polling booths, and election information.

🧾

2. Help desk

Guide people towards official registration and correction processes; avoid collecting unnecessary personal data.

📣

3. Awareness rally

Use simple messages and a voter pledge to spread awareness across the community.

🎭

4. EVM/VVPAT demo

Explain the voting process in an educational demonstration and use a street play to engage people.

A non-partisan message

Informed choices. Respectful participation.

Voter awareness should help people understand the process—not tell them which party or candidate to support. Use reliable information, respect different opinions, and protect people’s privacy.

✓
Share verified informationUse official election resources for current rules and details.
✓
Keep it non-partisanDo not pressure anyone or promote a political party.
✓
Respect privacyNever post voter IDs, phone numbers, or personal records publicly.
